# Academy of Science

Academy of Science is an independent AI research and development lab. We conduct original research and work with organizations to investigate, prototype, evaluate, and build intelligent systems. Our capabilities span AI and machine learning, with deep specialization in Explainable AI (XAI) and Physical AI.

Shankaranarayan N specializes in XAI; Satwik Ramsetti specializes in Physical AI. We apply these capabilities independently or together across medicine, sports and performance, defence and security, automated driving, robotics, and industrial and scientific AI. These are areas of active interest rather than limits on the work we undertake.

Our work includes independent research, commissioned R&D, prototypes and demonstrators, and AI evaluation and assurance. We publish research notes and project updates as the work develops.
