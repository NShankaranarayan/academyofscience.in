# Academy of Science

Academy of Science is an independent research lab working across explainable AI (XAI), computer vision, and embodied AI systems. We specialize in Automated Driving Systems (ADS), bringing perception, explanation, and physical action together. Our wider application interests include medical AI and defence, with attention to systems that can be understood, evaluated, and responsibly used.

The lab is currently maintained by Shankaranarayan N and Satwik Ramsetti. We publish research notes and project updates as the work develops.
