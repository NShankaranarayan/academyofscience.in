## Problem

In 2020, Indian Railways undertook a timetable exercise for its six principal Golden Quadrilateral plus Diagonals (GQD) routes, spanning more than 9,000 km. The paper describes adapting a rail-traffic simulator and its methods to the network, integrating operational data, and managing delivery challenges of a large first-of-its-kind simulation-supported timetabling effort.

## Approach

Satwik V. Ramisetty is one of the co-authors in a multidisciplinary team involving IIT Bombay, Railway Board, and the Centre for Railway Information Systems. The paper records the project approach, customization and integration challenges, and the combination of top-down and bottom-up planning.

## Findings

The reported outcome is a network-wide timetable exercise for the GQD routes using simulation. The article is a case study of implementation and project delivery; its abstract does not claim a quantified improvement in capacity, punctuality, or service performance.
