# Why explainability matters when AI acts

When an AI system influences a decision or acts in the physical world, performance alone is not enough. People need ways to inspect its behaviour, understand its limits, and decide when to trust or override it.

Academy of Science studies explainable AI and Physical AI, with applications ranging from medical AI to defence. This note is a starting point for our work on interpretability, evaluation, human oversight, and responsible deployment. It is a research direction, not a claim that the lab has completed a particular system or study.
