# Why explainability matters when AI acts

When an AI system influences a decision or acts in the physical world, performance alone is not enough. People need ways to inspect its behaviour, understand its limits, and decide when to trust or override it.

Explainable AI is one of the Academy's core specializations. This note is a starting point for our work on interpretability, uncertainty, evaluation, human oversight, and responsible deployment across different AI systems and application domains. It is a research direction, not a claim that the lab has completed a particular system or study.
