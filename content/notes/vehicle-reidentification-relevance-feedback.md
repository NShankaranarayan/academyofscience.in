## Problem

Vehicle re-identification searches for the same vehicle across a gallery of images, despite changes in camera angle, lighting, and appearance. This work proposes a content-based image retrieval approach using computationally efficient deep neural features, with relevance feedback as a post-processing step.

## Approach

The paper combines deep feature extraction with an image-retrieval workflow and adds user relevance feedback to refine returned candidates. Shankaranarayan N. and S. Sowmya Kamath are listed as authors.

## Findings

The published abstract reports significant improvement in mean average precision and Rank@k after relevance feedback. The abstract record does not provide metric values, so no numeric gain is stated here.
