Profile

Shankaranarayan develops and evaluates AI systems for complex, safety-critical settings. His work uses explanations to inspect model behaviour, identify influential evidence, understand failure modes, and support root-cause analysis. His research interests include interpretable foundation models, explainable-by-design methods, and clinically meaningful explanations for medical imaging.
